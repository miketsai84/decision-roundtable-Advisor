# Decision Roundtable Advisor

A five-persona decision roundtable that stress-tests any decision from five independent angles — then delivers one clear recommendation.

## What it is

`decision-roundtable` turns a single decision (a dilemma, trade-off, strategy question, or life / career / investment choice) into a structured debate among five distinct thinking styles. Each persona guards one gate every good decision must pass:

| Persona | Guards | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| **Elon Musk** | Feasibility & speed | Break it down to first principles and validate fast |
| **Nassim Taleb** | Survival | Don't predict; manage exposure. Stay in the game first |
| **Charlie Munger** | Judgment quality | Invert, check incentives, stay inside your circle of competence |
| **Carl Jung** | The decision-maker | Before *what to do*, ask *who is deciding, and why* |
| **Peter Drucker** | Effectiveness & results | Results live outside; ask the right question, then do the right thing — and decide what to abandon |

## How it works

1. **Framing** — the moderator states the decision, the options (including a middle path you didn't list), reversibility, time horizon, and worst case.
2. **Round 1** — each persona speaks (120–250 words) in their own voice, ending with a one-line stance. Speaking order shifts by topic (career → Jung first; organization/management → Drucker first).
3. **Round 2** — 2–4 real clashes where personas challenge each other directly, with at least one substantive concession.
4. **Moderator synthesis** — an explicit recommendation (not "it depends"), a confidence level, five checks (bankruptcy veto / inversion / bottleneck / motive / results & abandonment), a small reversible next step, and what evidence would change the answer.

## Design principles

- Five *different* lines of reasoning — not one voice wearing five names.
- Real disagreement, not manufactured drama; if they converge, the difference shows in reasoning, timing, and thresholds.
- Not every persona must fill the word count: when a framework barely applies, a short paragraph or one sharp question is enough.
- Honest boundaries: it is a **thinking framework**, not professional advice. For medical, legal, tax, or large-money decisions it tells you to consult a professional. Investment questions cover risk structure only — never buy/sell calls.

## Size variants

- **Quick** (~700–1,000 words) — small decisions, "keep it short".
- **Standard** (~2,500–3,500 words) — the default full format.
- **Deep** (4,500+ words) — reads the full persona files, adds a third round and a pre-mortem.

## Trigger

Say *"give me advice"* (給我建議) and the roundtable opens.
